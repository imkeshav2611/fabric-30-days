For engineers

What to know before you build

The SQL endpoint is read-onlyYou can query and create views, not insert or update. Data is written by Spark or by ingestion tools.
Only Delta shows up as a tableA folder of plain Parquet or CSV stays in Files until you load it into a Delta table.
Schemas organise tablesNew lakehouses created in the portal have schemas enabled, so group tables instead of one flat list.
Tables need careMany small writes create many small files. Plan compaction from the start.
The honest part

Is a Lakehouse the right start?

Good fit

  • Your team is comfortable with Spark or Python
  • Data arrives as files, semi-structured or large
  • You want one store for engineering and BI

Think twice

  • The team only knows SQL
  • You need multi-table transactions in T-SQL
  • Everything is small and already relational

The catch: because the endpoint is read-only, SQL-first teams often feel stuck on day two. Decide who writes the data, and with what, before choosing.
