The problem

Power BI always made you pick a compromise.

1ImportFast, but data is a copy and goes stale
2DirectQueryAlways fresh, but every click is slow

Speed or freshness. For years you could not have both.

The simple version

Reading the book in the library.

Import is photocopying the whole library and carrying it home. DirectQuery is phoning the librarian with every question.

Direct Lake is walking in and reading the book off the shelf: no copy, no middleman.

How it works

What happens when you open a report

1The model points at Delta tables in OneLake
2Only the columns the visual needs are loaded
3They go straight into memory for fast analysis
4Framing moves the model to the latest table version
Why it is fastDelta's Parquet files are already columnar. The engine reads them as they are instead of converting them.
For engineers

The two flavours

Direct Lake on OneLakeReads Delta tables directly. Can combine several sources and mix with Import tables. No DirectQuery fallback.
Direct Lake on SQLGoes through a SQL analytics endpoint. Falls back to DirectQuery when it has to, which is slower.
Guardrails depend on capacity sizeEach SKU has limits on rows and files per table. Past them, one flavour falls back and the other fails.
Table health shows up hereMany small files and missing V-Order make Direct Lake slower.
The honest part

Direct Lake or Import?

Direct Lake

  • Data already lives in OneLake as Delta
  • Tables are large or refreshed often
  • You want one copy of the data

Import

  • Small models where import is simple
  • Sources outside Fabric
  • Heavy use of calculated columns

The catch: Direct Lake is only as fast as the Delta tables under it. A table with thousands of small files turns a fast mode into a slow one.

Watch out

Four things to check

1Which flavour your model uses, and whether it can fall back
2Guardrails for your capacity size
3V-Order and compaction on the tables reports read
4Security: SQL row-level rules do not apply to the OneLake flavour

Direct Lake rewards good table engineering more than any other mode.
