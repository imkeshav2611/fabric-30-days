What changes for you

It is SaaS, not PaaSNo clusters to build or patch. Spark starter pools give you a session in seconds.
One open format: Delta ParquetSpark writes a table. The SQL endpoint and Power BI read that same table. No export step.
One compute currencyEvery workload spends Capacity Units from the same F SKU. Sizing is one decision, not six.
Shortcuts instead of copiesPoint OneLake at ADLS, S3 or Google Cloud Storage and query the data where it sits.
The honest part

Is Fabric right for you?

Good fit

  • Your company already lives in Power BI and Azure
  • A small team that wants less infrastructure work
  • You need batch, real-time and reports in one place

Think twice

  • You need fine control over cluster configuration
  • You are deep in another platform that works well
  • Nobody will watch capacity usage

The catch people miss: capacity is shared. One heavy Spark job can slow down everybody's reports. Plan your capacity before you plan your pipelines.
