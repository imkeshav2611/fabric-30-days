For engineers

What engineers should know

Query folding decides the speedWhen steps can be pushed down to the source, it is fast. When they cannot, rows are pulled and processed in the engine.
Staging has a costStaging helps large transformations but adds compute. Turn it off for simple loads.
Fast copy for big movesLarge, simple ingestion can skip row-by-row processing.
Git and CI/CD are built inSince April 2026 every new dataflow is created with Git and deployment support, like other code.
The honest part

Dataflow or notebook?

Dataflow

  • Small to medium data
  • Analysts own the logic
  • Many sources with simple shaping

Notebook

  • Very large tables
  • Complex logic that needs tests
  • Tight control over performance

The catch: a dataflow that was fine at one million rows can struggle at one hundred million. Watch the refresh time, and move heavy steps to Spark before they hurt.

Watch out

Four things to watch

1Steps that break folding early in the query
2Staging left on for loads that do not need it
3One giant dataflow instead of small, reusable ones
4No owner once the analyst who built it moves on

Low-code is still code. It needs the same care.
