The honest part

What Fabric is, and is not

It gives you

  • One platform instead of six services
  • One copy of the data in an open format
  • Batch, real time, BI and AI together

You still bring

  • A clear data model and definitions
  • Ownership for every important table
  • The discipline to monitor and maintain

The honest summary: Fabric removes the plumbing between tools. It does not remove the engineering. Good platforms are still designed, not purchased.
