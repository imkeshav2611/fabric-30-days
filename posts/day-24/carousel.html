For engineers

Making it work in practice

Certify sparinglyIf everything is Certified, the badge means nothing. Reserve it for data a named owner stands behind.
Lineage is per workspaceThe lineage view covers one workspace. Cross-workspace flows need more deliberate tracking.
Labels inheritA label on a lakehouse carries through to items built on it, which protects exports too.
Ownership before toolingA catalog full of items with no owner is a tidy list of unanswered questions.
The honest part

How much governance is enough?

Start with

  • A named owner for every Gold table
  • Sensitivity labels on personal data
  • Certification for core datasets

Add later

  • A committee for every change
  • Detailed documentation of Bronze
  • Approval steps nobody will follow

The catch: governance that slows people down gets bypassed. The version that works is the lightest one people will actually follow.
