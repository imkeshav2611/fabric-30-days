Microsoft Fabric, Day 16 of 30: Real-Time Intelligence.

"Real time" is one of the most over-used phrases in data. It is worth being precise about it.

Batch processing collects data and handles it later, which answers "what happened". Streaming handles each event as it arrives, which answers "what is happening". Real-Time Intelligence is the part of Fabric built for the second kind of question.

It has five building blocks. Real-Time hub is where you discover streams. Eventstream brings events in and shapes them. Eventhouse stores them and makes them queryable at very large scale. Real-Time Dashboards show the current state. Activator takes an action when a condition is met.

The question I ask first is not technical: who will act on this, and how quickly? If the answer is "tomorrow morning", a batch pipeline is cheaper and simpler. Real time earns its cost when minutes matter.

I worked on a real-time platform for a consumer delivery business, where streaming data arrived at terabytes per minute through Event Hub and Eventstream, and Eventhouse served sub-second analytics. The first production use case shipped in under 14 days.
