For engineers

How to think about it

Events, not tablesA stream is an endless series of small messages. You query windows of time, not a finished dataset.
Latency is a requirement, not a defaultAsk how fresh the answer really needs to be. Seconds, minutes and hours are very different builds.
It can meet batch in OneLakeEvent data can be exposed as Delta tables, so streaming and batch data can be joined.
Different capacity behaviourStreaming runs all day. Budget for steady usage, not occasional peaks.
The honest part

Do you need real time?

Yes when

  • Someone acts on the data within minutes
  • Sensors, clicks or logs arrive nonstop
  • Delay costs money or safety

Probably not when

  • Decisions are made daily or weekly
  • The source only updates overnight
  • Nobody is watching the screen

The catch: real time is expensive to run and easy to over-buy. If nobody acts faster because of it, an hourly batch gives the same outcome for much less.
