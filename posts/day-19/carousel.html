For engineers

Designing rules that people trust

Alert on change, not on state"Temperature crossed 80" fires once. "Temperature is above 80" fires all afternoon.
Many things can feed a ruleEventstreams, KQL queries, Power BI visuals, dashboards and Fabric events can all trigger Activator.
Close the loop automaticallyThe best rule does not page a person. It starts the job that fixes the problem.
Live refresh saves computeTiles re-query when new data is ingested, instead of on a fixed timer.
The honest part

Alert or just a report?

Set an alert

  • Someone must respond within minutes
  • The condition is clear and measurable
  • There is a defined next step

A report is enough

  • The number is reviewed weekly
  • Nobody knows what to do when it fires
  • The threshold is a guess

The catch: alert fatigue. Ten alerts a day and people mute the channel. One meaningful alert a week gets acted on. Fewer, sharper rules win.
