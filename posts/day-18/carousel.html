For engineers

Settings that control cost and speed

Caching policyDecides how much recent data is kept on fast storage. Hot data is fast, cold data is cheaper.
Retention policyDecides how long data is kept at all. Set it per table, on purpose.
OneLake availabilityTurn it on and the same data appears as Delta tables for Spark, SQL and Power BI.
Minimum capacityAn eventhouse can be kept always on, with a scheduled minimum size, to avoid slow first queries.
The honest part

Eventhouse or Lakehouse?

Eventhouse

  • Time-stamped events at high volume
  • Queries about the last minutes or hours
  • Free-text or semi-structured data

Lakehouse

  • Large batch transformations
  • Data updated row by row
  • Classic star-schema reporting

The catch: default retention and caching are rarely right for your data. Leave them alone and you either pay for history nobody queries or lose data someone needed.
