How it works

Where to look

1Monitoring hub: every run of every pipeline, notebook and refresh
2Capacity Metrics app: who used how much compute
3Workspace monitoring: detailed logs you can query with KQL

Three questionsDid it run, is it getting slower, and is it eating the capacity? Answer those and you cover most incidents.
For engineers

What each tool is good for

Monitoring hub is your first stopIt lists runs across the workspace with status and duration. Start every investigation here.
The Metrics app looks back 14 daysCompute history covers 14 days and storage 30. Longer trends need your own copy.
The Metrics app has no alertsTo be warned about capacity, use capacity events in Real-Time hub with Activator.
Surge protection is a safety netIt limits background work when the capacity is under pressure, protecting interactive users.
The honest part

What deserves an alert?

Alert on

  • A production pipeline failing
  • A refresh taking twice as long as usual
  • Capacity nearing its limit

Just log

  • Every successful run
  • Failures in development workspaces
  • Warnings nobody acts on

The catch: a job that succeeds can still be wrong. It ran, it wrote zero rows, and everything is green. Monitor the data, not only the job.

Watch out

Four checks that catch most problems

1Freshness: when did this table last get new rows?
2Volume: is today's row count in the normal range?
3Duration: is the job slower than last week?
4Capacity: what was running when throttling began?

Good monitoring is four questions, asked automatically.
